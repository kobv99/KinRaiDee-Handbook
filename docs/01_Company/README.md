# Company Foundation

## Vision

KinRaiDee aims to become a trusted Kitchen Operating System that helps households make better food decisions every day.

The product should eventually understand what is available, what should be used soon, what can be cooked, what should be purchased, and how food choices affect cost, waste, repetition, and nutrition.

## Mission

Help people manage their kitchens with less waste, less uncertainty, and less unnecessary spending by combining reliable pantry data, practical cooking workflows, and optional AI assistance.

## North Star

> Make the next useful kitchen decision obvious.

Every major product decision should improve at least one of these outcomes:

- the user knows what they have;
- the user knows what to cook;
- the user wastes less food;
- the user buys more intentionally;
- the user can trust inventory changes;
- the user spends less time planning routine meals.

## Company principles

### Offline first

Core pantry, recipe, cooking, history, and shopping workflows should remain useful without an internet connection.

### Privacy first

Household pantry and cooking history are personal behavioural data. Collection, sync, and AI use must be intentional and transparent.

### User data ownership

Users should be able to retain, export, and eventually migrate their data. Cloud services must enhance ownership rather than create lock-in.

### AI second

AI may reason over reliable product data, but it must not replace deterministic inventory rules or become a required dependency for basic product value.

### Practical value over feature count

A feature is valuable only when it reduces a real burden in the kitchen. Technology novelty alone is not a product justification.

### Sustainable engineering

We optimise for a system that can keep evolving. Quick delivery is useful only when it does not destroy correctness, testability, or future options.

## Team roles

### CEO / Product Owner

Owns product direction, user validation, prioritisation, and business decisions.

### CTO

Owns technical strategy, architecture, roadmap translation, engineering standards, risk management, and documentation integrity.

### Principal Software Engineer / Codex

Owns implementation quality, tests, branch discipline, clear technical reporting, and faithful execution of approved specifications.

## Constitution

1. Pantry data is the operational source of truth for available ingredients.
2. Inventory mutations must be explicit, validated, and auditable.
3. AI may advise but must not silently mutate trusted household data.
4. Business rules belong in testable domain code, not UI widgets.
5. Important product and architecture decisions must be documented.
6. A feature is not complete until its behaviour is validated and its documentation reflects reality.
7. The system must preserve a path toward offline use and user data ownership.
8. New complexity must justify itself through measurable user value or reduced engineering risk.
